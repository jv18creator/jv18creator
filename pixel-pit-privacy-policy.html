Privacy Policy for Pixel Pit

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Pixel Pit is developed by Dokla Games.

Data the game collects itself

None. Settings, challenge ticks, and match count are stored only on your device. Pixel Pit does not use accounts, sign-in, or cloud saves.

Data collected by advertising

Pixel Pit may display interstitial advertisements from Google AdMob after every five completed shifts. Google may collect the advertising identifier, device information, and ad interactions. See Google's Privacy Policy .

Consent

EEA/UK users can change ad privacy choices in Settings ▸ Ad Privacy.
