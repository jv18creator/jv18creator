Privacy Policy for Arrow Snake

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Arrow Snake is developed by Dokla Games. This page explains what data the game and its service providers handle, and the choices you have.

Data the game handles

  • Anonymous player ID. The game signs you in anonymously with Unity Gaming Services so your progress can be saved. No name or email is asked for.
  • Game progress. Level, coins, boosters, daily streak and awards are stored on your device and backed up with Unity Cloud Save under your player ID.
  • Google Play Games account (optional). If you choose Connect Account, your Play Games player ID is linked to your game profile so progress can move to a new device.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase receipt to deliver what you bought; we never see your payment details.
  • Gameplay analytics (only with your consent). Unity Analytics records events such as level started, completed or failed, boosters used, ads watched and purchases, together with device model, OS version and country.
  • Daily puzzle reminders (optional). If you allow notifications, the game schedules a reminder on your device for when a new daily puzzle is available. Reminders are created locally on your device; no data is sent to us or to third parties for them.

Advertising

Arrow Snake shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. These providers may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to show and measure ads. If you do not consent, ads are not personalised. See the Unity Privacy Policy and the LevelPlay (ironSource) Privacy Policy. Purchasing Remove Ads stops ads between levels; optional reward ads stay available.

Your choices

On first launch the game asks whether you allow personalised ads and analytics. You can change your answer at any time in Me > Privacy Preferences. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings. Daily puzzle reminders can be turned off at any time in Me > Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Data deletion

To delete your game data (player profile, cloud save and analytics), email us from the contact address below with the subject "Arrow Snake data deletion" and your player ID, shown under Me > About. We delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.

Children

Arrow Snake is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Terms of use

Arrow Snake is provided as is. Coins and other virtual items have no cash value, cannot be exchanged or transferred, and are licensed to you for use in the game only. Refunds for purchases follow Google Play's refund policy. We may update the game and these terms; continued use means you accept the current version on this page.
